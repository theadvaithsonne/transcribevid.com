# Asking Google to index every page: a day-by-day checklist

For you, the owner. Google lets you press **Request indexing** for only about 10 addresses a day, so the pages are split into
days, most useful first. Start on **Wednesday 28 October 2026** and tick each box.

**How to do one address (1 minute):**
1. Search Console, search bar at the top ("Inspect any URL"), paste the full address, for example
   `https://transcribevid.com/speech-to-text`, press Enter.
2. If it says **URL is on Google**, you are done: tick the box and go to the next one.
3. If it says **URL is not on Google**, press **Request indexing** and wait for "Indexing requested" (about a minute).
4. If Google says you have used the daily quota, stop. Do the rest tomorrow.

Already requested earlier (skip): `/`, `/video-to-text`, `/audio-to-text`, `/mp4-to-text`, `/mp3-to-text`, `/guides`, `/es`.
If you requested other addresses that are not in the days below, simply skip them when you reach them.

The 510 translated pages do not need requests: Google finds them through the sitemap and the language menu. Only the
translated home pages are on the list, so each language gets a first page that Google has seen.

## After Day 6: what to watch

- **Search Console, Pages:** the number under *Indexed* should grow over 1 to 6 weeks. Do not worry about *Page with redirect* or
  *Not found (404)* for addresses you never made.
- **Search Console, Performance:** the first impressions usually appear within 1 to 3 weeks of a page being indexed, and the first
  clicks after that. Send me screenshots of *Pages* and *Performance* a week after Day 6.
- **Do not request the same address twice.** It does not speed anything up.
- **Bing:** at https://www.bing.com/webmasters choose *Import from Google Search Console* once; it copies the site and the sitemap.
- **Links matter more than requests.** Requesting indexing only gets Google to look. Whether a page ranks depends mostly on
  other websites linking to it: use the ready-made text in `deploy/LISTINGS.md` alongside this plan.

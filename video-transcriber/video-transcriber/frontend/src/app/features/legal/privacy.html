{{ site.brand().name }} turns video and audio into text. This page explains what that involves for your data, in plain words. In short: your files and transcripts are kept only for your session, there is no account, no tracking and no advertising.

1. Who runs this website

{{ site.brand().name }} ({{ site.brand().domain }}) is operated by {{ op.name }}, {{ op.address }} ("we", "us"). We decide how and why your data is processed, as described here.

2. What we process

  • Media you add. The video or audio files you upload or record, so that the speech in them can be recognised.
  • Text we generate. The transcript, its timestamps and your edits, the title you choose, and the settings you pick (quality, language, vocabulary hint).
  • Technical logs. Basic operating information such as the time of a request, what was requested, the result or error, file sizes, a random session identifier and your IP address. They never contain the content of your media or transcripts. We use them to run the service, fix errors and prevent abuse (for example, rate limiting).
  • One running total. We keep one anonymous running total of how many files have been transcribed on the site (a single number, shown in the page header). It is not linked to you, your files or your session.

We do not ask for your name, e-mail address or any other account details.

3. Why we process it

To give you the transcription you asked for, and to keep the service secure and available for everyone. Where data protection law such as the GDPR applies, our legal bases are providing the service you requested (Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in security and preventing abuse (Art. 6(1)(f)).

4. How long we keep it

  • Files and transcripts exist only for your session. They are deleted automatically when it ends, which happens after @if (site.idleText()) { {{ site.idleText() }} } @else { a period } of inactivity, or at once when you press Delete my data now in the Transcripts menu.
  • Apart from that single number, nothing is stored permanently. We do not keep copies or backups of your files or transcripts, and a restart of the service also clears them.
  • Technical logs are kept only for a limited time for operations and abuse prevention, and contain no media or transcript content.

Because your transcripts exist only in your browser session, export what you need before you leave. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

5. Cookies and browser storage

  • One essential session cookie. It holds a random session identifier so that your files and transcripts stay separate from other visitors' and so that the service works at all. It is strictly necessary, so there is no cookie banner.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers on this website.
  • Your browser remembers your light or dark theme choice, and its session storage keeps a few convenience settings for the current tab (such as your last quality choice). They stay in your browser and are not sent to us.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not share your files or transcripts with third parties. The recognition runs on our own server, not on an external transcription or AI service. If hosting or network providers are involved, they only act as technical service providers on our behalf. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

7. Security

Data travels between your browser and our server over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and each session is kept separate from other visitors' sessions. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not upload anything you are not comfortable processing this way.

8. Your rights and choices

Because we hold no account and no details that identify you, we usually cannot link stored data to a person.
